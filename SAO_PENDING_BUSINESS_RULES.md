# SAO Pending Business Rules

Updated: 2026-10-15. This register distinguishes resolved agreements from administrator-defined parameters and unsupported capabilities. No isolated fixture values are approved production defaults.

## Resolved agreements — do not reopen or weaken

1. SAO automatically plans/builds/tests safe reversible work. High-risk actions need Supervisor approval; spending and irreversible actions need genuine business-owner authorization.
2. Routine low-risk pricing may be automatic within configured boundaries. Pricing, Marketing and Finance collaborate; major changes need Supervisor approval.
3. The owner authorizes 20% of verified positive net profit weekly. Within-scope verified settlements need no invented repeat consent; disputes and out-of-scope transfers pause.
4. Serious risk permits immediate suspension. Permanent closure is separately governed; irreversible deletion needs owner approval. Financial/customer obligations survive.
5. Opportunity priority uses demand, severity, evidence and solution potential. Complexity affects reasoning and expertise, not priority.
6. QC is independent; Architect separately verifies specification compliance. Neither can override mandatory failed QC.
7. Eligible profit deducts legitimate expenses, refunds, processing fees and applicable taxes, then prior losses. SAO receives 20%, business 80%, only on positive eligible profit.

## Administrator-defined configuration still required

- Discovery: independent source count (at least two), evidence age, four ranking weights, qualification threshold, legal jurisdiction/review evidence and applicable reasoning/model capabilities.
- Specification: required sections and permission for safe future-backlog improvements. Approved versions, Architect review, conflict escalation and current-version enforcement are mandatory.
- Agents: allowed tool scopes, original/backup retry counts, available backup agents and supported model assignments. Owner/Supervisor authority cannot be replaced with caller assertions.
- Pricing: automatic-change permission, adjustment percentage and minimum interval. Customer commitments and completed transaction prices remain protected.
- QC: mandatory supported suites, performance acceptance and repair retry limits. Independent provenance, artifact hash and Architect compliance remain mandatory.
- Advertising: campaign budget ceiling and performance review interval, actual owner amount/currency/scope/expiry and separate reinvestment consent. Organic plans are not published campaigns.
- Finance: supported currency, IANA timezone, weekly cutoff, supported cash/verified-received convention, explicit verified tax/fee treatment, rounding, accounting start and opening losses. Exact owner 20%-weekly agreement required.
- Deadlines: industry, complexity, initial days, warning days, profitability threshold/periods, growth/demand/sustainability criteria, extension days/count and fixed-price boundaries.
- Shutdown: retention period, loss-suspension threshold and legitimate retention/deletion obligations.
- System: public HTTPS origin, durable backup storage/off-instance copy, notification destinations and existing credential/provider configuration.

## Supported implementation boundaries

- Cash accounting with explicitly configured two-decimal ISO currency is implemented. Accrual, foreign-exchange conversion and crypto-to-fiat reconciliation are unavailable and fail closed; do not relabel nominal fiat invoice values as verified cash.
- Payout execution requires a real supported transfer/reconciliation adapter. Eligible calculations remain transfer_unavailable, never paid.
- External advertising/organic publication requires a genuine adapter and appropriate authorization. Internal plans must not be labelled published.
- Artifact QC currently supports self-contained static applications with executable Firefox acceptance/security/performance checks. Generic backend/mobile runners and a comprehensive security audit are not supplied.
- Retention controls review eligibility; automatic irreversible customer/resource deletion is unavailable.
- External outreach requires lawful source/consent and a supported authorized execution path; no fabricated customer acquisition.
- Production administrator permissions, migrations and discovery reset each need their appropriate explicit authorization. Existing Northflank is never disposable.
